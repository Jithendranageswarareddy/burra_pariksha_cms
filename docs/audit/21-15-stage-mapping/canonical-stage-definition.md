## 1. The Canonical 15-Stage Business Reference

The canonical workflow reference comprises exactly 15 sequential business stages:

1. **01 Question Generation:** Ideation, AI prompt engineering, syllabus taxonomy mapping, Telugu question drafting.
2. **02 Question Verification:** 10-point pedagogical verification, mathematical proof audit, SME sign-off, video queueing.
3. **03 Audience Script:** Short-form presenter script with 3-second curiosity hook, 3-step solution, and speed trick.
4. **04 Teleprompter & Filming:** Presenter studio recording session with auto-scrolling teleprompter and multi-take logging.
5. **05 Raw Video:** Ingestion of camera footage files into Google Drive, checksum capture, and video entity linking.
6. **06 Editing Bay:** Master timeline cut, dynamic Telugu subtitles, sound effects, timer overlays, and render generation.
7. **07 Final QC:** 6-point Master QC certification (1080x1920 9:16 safe-zones, audio LUFS, Telugu typo check).
8. **08 Thumbnail:** High-CTR curiosity framing graphic design, mobile preview, and 5MB Drive asset linking.
9. **09 Social Review:** 9:16 smartphone simulator audit, packaging, tags, hashtags, and pinned comment sign-off.
10. **10 Publishing Setup:** Multi-platform scheduling, platform slot configuration, and pre-publish readiness confirmation.
11. **11 Published / Live:** Live publication on YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and Facebook Video with regex URL checks.
12. **12 Platform Sync:** Verification of cross-platform post adaptations, character limits, audio attributions, and sync status.
13. **13 Analytics:** Collection and recording of 24h and 7d audience engagement metrics (views, retention %, likes, comments).
14. **14 Performance Review:** Retention curve drop-off diagnosis, student confusion identification, and editorial critique.
15. **15 Intelligence Loop:** AI pedagogical insights synthesis, topic recommendation generation, and loopback to Stage 01.
