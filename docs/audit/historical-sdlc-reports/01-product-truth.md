# Burra Pariksha CMS
# Stage 1 — Product & Workflow Truth

---

## 1. Product Objective

### What is Burra Pariksha CMS?
Burra Pariksha CMS is a specialized, production-grade content manufacturing and performance intelligence operating system for competitive examination preparation media. It operates as an industrial-standard digital assembly line, transforming raw syllabus domains and academic problem concepts into verified, produced, distributed, and measured short-form and long-form educational video assets.

### Primary Purpose
The primary purpose of Burra Pariksha CMS is to enforce zero-defect quality gates across the end-to-end content lifecycle, ensuring that every published question, script, video, thumbnail, and social package is academically accurate, pedagogically sound, broadcast-ready, and continuously optimized through empirical audience intelligence.

### What is the Production System Responsible For?
The production system is strictly responsible for:
1. **Curricular & Problem Genesis**: Ingesting or generating structured examination questions across quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, and general studies.
2. **Deterministic Academic Verification**: Enforcing multi-criteria review (mathematical validity, syllabus alignment, unambiguous phrasing, single definitive correct answer) before production capital is spent.
3. **Studio Production Orchestration**: Driving script delivery, teleprompter pacing, on-camera recording, raw asset handoff, video editing, and technical quality control (QC).
4. **Packaging & Packaging Verification**: Assembling high-CTR thumbnails, platform adaptations, spoken audio tags, and pinned engagement commentary.
5. **Distribution Control**: Managing scheduling, upload integrity, and post-publish live URL verification across external networks (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook).
6. **Closed-Loop Intelligence**: Ingesting social telemetry, evaluating content engagement and retention, and feeding empirical findings back into future topic selection and difficulty calibration.

### The Lifecycle of One Content Item
A single content item originates as an educational question concept (Step 01). It moves sequentially through formal verification (Step 02), presentation preparation (Step 03), physical filming (Step 04), raw footage ingestion (Step 05), post-production editing (Step 06), rigorous audio/visual/academic QC (Step 07), packaging design (Step 08), holistic social package review (Step 09), platform staging (Step 10), live release verification (Step 11), cross-platform synchronization (Step 12), engagement metric harvesting (Step 13), editorial performance assessment (Step 14), and strategic intelligence derivation (Step 15) to seed subsequent questions.

### Meaning of Core Lifecycle Milestones
- **"Content Completed"**: The editorial and creative work (question text, video file, thumbnail mockup, pinned comment) is finalized, passes Technical QC (Step 07), and passes Social Review (Step 09). It is fully packaged and ready for distribution.
- **"Production Completed"**: The asset package has been deployed to live distribution platforms, verified as publicly accessible with functional audio/video (Step 11), and syndicated across all designated target channels (Step 12).
- **"Performance Intelligence"**: The extraction of actionable editorial and pedagogical conclusions from aggregated retention, view velocity, comment comprehension, and drop-off analytics.
- **How One Completed Content Item Contributes to the Next**: Step 15 analyzes the audience engagement, failure rate, and comment inquiries from completed items. If an item reveals common student misconceptions, high drop-off at specific problem steps, or exceptional virality on a subtopic, Step 15 generates a high-priority "Next-Content Directive" specifying topics, difficulty adjustments, and script hook patterns for Step 01 of future production batches.

## 2. Production Item

The conceptual object that flows through the 15 production stages is the **Production Item**. It is an immutable-identity work bundle containing the entire historical and evolving ledger of a single educational content asset.

Conceptually, a Production Item encapsulates:

1. **Identity & Taxonomy Dossier**:
   - Universal Content Identifier (canonical tracking identity).
   - Domain Classification: Target Exam, Category, Topic, Subtopic, Complexity Tier (e.g., Level 1 Foundation to Level 5 Advanced).

2. **Academic Question Package**:
   - Primary Question Stem (bilingual / target language representation).
   - Option Set (4 standardized distinct alternatives, mutually exclusive).
   - Definitive Correct Key & Detailed Canonical Solution / Step-by-Step Proof.
   - Conceptual Distractor Rationale (explaining why each wrong option exists).

3. **Academic Verification Record**:
   - Sign-off timestamp, verifier identity, and verified criteria checklist (Calculations Checked, Syllabus Mapped, No Ambiguity).
   - Verification status history (Approved, Rejected, Revision Requested with corrective comments).

4. **Presentation & Spoken Script**:
   - Verbal Hook (first 3 seconds attention anchor).
   - Spoken Explanation (teleprompter text formatted for natural speaking rhythm, pacing cues, pronunciation keys).
   - Visual Cue Directives (on-screen text, graphics, math formula pop-ups).

5. **Production Filming Dossier**:
   - Recording session metadata (talent/educator, shoot date, studio room, teleprompter speed preset).
   - Session take log (identifying selected take vs discarded takes).

6. **Raw Footage Vault Asset**:
   - Raw master video/audio file pointer and ingest verification.
   - Camera format, duration, audio levels check, and checksum verification.

7. **Edited Master Video**:
   - Cut and assembled master video (rendered to delivery specs: aspect ratio 9:16 or 16:9, resolution, framerate).
   - Burned-in caption stream, motion graphic overlays, sound effects, audio normalization (-14 LUFS).

8. **Quality Control (QC) Certificate**:
   - Verification of zero visual glitches, correct question/option matching, typo-free text, lip-sync alignment, and loudness compliance.
   - Formal pass/fail sign-off.

9. **Thumbnail & Presentation Packaging**:
   - Graphic asset (high contrast, mobile-optimized typography, subject facial expression, hook headline).
   - A/B thumbnail variations if applicable.

10. **Social Review Bundle**:
    - Complete review package combining Edited Video, Final Thumbnail, Title/Hook, Description, and Pinned Comment.
    - Editorial approval sign-off.

11. **Distribution Staging & Schedule Dossier**:
    - Platform-specific targeting: YouTube Shorts / Video, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels.
    - Publication timestamps, category tags, localized titles, engagement CTA.
    - Pinned comment content (follow-up question, community poll, or solution link).

12. **Live Deployment Verification Record**:
    - Live external URLs for each published target.
    - Immediate playback check confirmation (audio working, video crisp, correct aspect ratio, correct description).

13. **Cross-Platform Syndication Manifest**:
    - Verification of asset replication, audio library pairing, and cross-platform publishing consistency across secondary channels.

14. **Harvested Audience Telemetry Dossier**:
    - 24-hour, 7-day, and 30-day views, impressions, click-through rate (CTR), average percentage viewed (APV), retention curve drop-off points, likes, shares, saves, and comments.

15. **Performance Intelligence Evaluation**:
    - Qualitative and quantitative performance rating.
    - Student comprehension signal (analysis of comment section mistakes).
    - Directive for subsequent content iteration (e.g., "Students failed on step 3 fractions; schedule follow-up micro-breakdown").

## 3. Canonical 15-Step Workflow

The 15 canonical steps are strictly sequential under normal execution, forming a continuous unidirectional pipeline:

```
[01] Question Generation
       ↓
[02] Question Verification
       ↓
[03] Script / Teleprompter
       ↓
[04] Recording
       ↓
[05] Raw Footage Handoff
       ↓
[06] Editing
       ↓
[07] Final QC
       ↓
[08] Thumbnail
       ↓
[09] Social Review
       ↓
[10] Publishing Setup
       ↓
[11] Published / Live Verification
       ↓
[12] Platform Sync / Package
       ↓
[13] Social Analytics
       ↓
[14] Performance Review
       ↓
[15] Performance Intelligence / Next Question
       ↓
(Loops back to Step 01 with strategic directives)
```

## 4. Detailed Definition of All 15 Steps

---

### STEP 01 — Question Generation

1. **PURPOSE**: Formulate academically rigorous, curriculum-aligned questions and complete multiple-choice options with explanations to populate the production backlog.
2. **ACTOR / OWNER**: Question Creator / Curriculum Specialist.
3. **ENTRY CONDITION**: Topic, Subtopic, and Target Examination syllabus requirements exist; or a Performance Intelligence Directive (from Step 15) requests specific topic coverage.
4. **ENTRY POINT**: Question Creation Workboard.
5. **INPUTS**: Syllabus specifications, Exam Pattern blueprints, Topic parameters, historical performance directives.
6. **USER ACTIONS**: Author or generate question text, designate correct option, draft 3 plausible distractor options, write comprehensive step-by-step solution, tag taxonomy, and submit to verification.
7. **OUTPUT**: Draft Question Record with primary key, four options, correct answer key, and proof solution.
8. **COMPLETION CONDITION**: Question text, options A-D, single correct answer, step-by-step explanation, and taxonomy tags are fully populated and submitted for review.
9. **NEXT STEP**: Step 02 (Question Verification).
10. **REVISION / REJECTION**: N/A (genesis step).
11. **DATA CONSUMED**: Taxonomy standards, exam blueprint criteria, intelligence directives.
12. **DATA PRODUCED**: Unverified Question Record (Draft status).
13. **HANDOFF**: Draft Question Package containing complete problem, choices, and explanation.
14. **RESPONSIBLE ROLE**: Question Creator.
15. **BUSINESS RULES**: Every question must have exactly one unambiguously correct answer; distractor options must reflect authentic common calculation or reasoning errors; question must not duplicate existing repository items.

---

### STEP 02 — Question Verification

1. **PURPOSE**: Ensure absolute mathematical, grammatical, factual, and syllabus correctness before committing studio resources.
2. **ACTOR / OWNER**: Academic Reviewer / Verification Specialist.
3. **ENTRY CONDITION**: Step 01 complete; Question is in `Pending Verification` status.
4. **ENTRY POINT**: Question Verification Workboard.
5. **INPUTS**: Draft Question Package from Step 01.
6. **USER ACTIONS**: Independently solve the problem, check mathematical proof, evaluate option distinctness, verify difficulty tier, ensure no linguistic ambiguity, and either approve or reject with specific corrective notes.
7. **OUTPUT**: Signed Academic Verification Record (Approved or Rejected).
8. **COMPLETION CONDITION**: Verifier formally submits an affirmative verification decision certifying all verification criteria are met.
9. **NEXT STEP**: Step 03 (Script / Teleprompter) if approved; Step 01 if rejected.
10. **REVISION / REJECTION**: Rejection routes the item back to Step 01 with structured deficiency notes; item cannot proceed forward until re-submitted and verified.
11. **DATA CONSUMED**: Draft Question Record.
12. **DATA PRODUCED**: Verified Question Record, Verifier Sign-off stamp, or Rejection Reason Log.
13. **HANDOFF**: Verified Question Package certified for scripting.
14. **RESPONSIBLE ROLE**: Academic Reviewer.
15. **BUSINESS RULES**: Verification cannot be performed by the same individual who authored the question (two-person rule); partial approvals are prohibited.

---

### STEP 03 — Script / Teleprompter

1. **PURPOSE**: Translate the academic question and solution into an engaging, conversational, broadcast-ready teleprompter script optimized for vertical video retention.
2. **ACTOR / OWNER**: Script Writer.
3. **ENTRY CONDITION**: Step 02 complete; Question is in `Verified` status.
4. **ENTRY POINT**: Script Production Workboard.
5. **INPUTS**: Verified Question Package (Question stem, correct key, step-by-step explanation).
6. **USER ACTIONS**: Structure the verbal hook (0-3 seconds), draft teleprompter prose emphasizing the critical solving trick/concept, add on-screen visual overlay cues (e.g., `[SHOW FORMULA]`), adjust pacing to target length (under 60s for short-form), and lock the script.
7. **OUTPUT**: Approved Teleprompter Script with visual cue markers and timing estimates.
8. **COMPLETION CONDITION**: Script has complete hook, body, and conclusion formatted for teleprompter display, reviewed and locked for studio delivery.
9. **NEXT STEP**: Step 04 (Recording).
10. **REVISION / REJECTION**: If the script fails creative or timing standards, it is revised within Step 03. If a hidden academic flaw is discovered, it is escalated back to Step 02.
11. **DATA CONSUMED**: Verified Question Package.
12. **DATA PRODUCED**: Production Script Record (Hook, Spoken Text, Visual Directives, Target Duration).
13. **HANDOFF**: Locked Teleprompter Script ready for the recording educator.
14. **RESPONSIBLE ROLE**: Script Writer.
15. **BUSINESS RULES**: The spoken script must not alter the mathematical substance of the verified question; language must remain natural, concise, and energetic.

---

### STEP 04 — Recording

1. **PURPOSE**: Film and record the presenter delivering the verified script on camera with high fidelity audio and video.
2. **ACTOR / OWNER**: Presenter / Studio Recording Operator.
3. **ENTRY CONDITION**: Step 03 complete; Teleprompter Script is locked.
4. **ENTRY POINT**: Studio Recording Workboard / Teleprompter Stage.
5. **INPUTS**: Locked Teleprompter Script, Presenter Guidelines, Production Session Schedule.
6. **USER ACTIONS**: Mount teleprompter, set lighting and microphone, deliver on-camera performance, log successful take number, verify audio clarity, and conclude session.
7. **OUTPUT**: Recorded Takes logged with designated Hero Take selection.
8. **COMPLETION CONDITION**: Presenter and Studio Operator sign off that an acceptable, error-free hero take was successfully captured on camera.
9. **NEXT STEP**: Step 05 (Raw Footage Handoff).
10. **REVISION / REJECTION**: In-session retakes occur immediately during Step 04. If the shoot is aborted, the session is rescheduled.
11. **DATA CONSUMED**: Teleprompter Script.
12. **DATA PRODUCED**: Recording Session Log, Hero Take ID, Raw Clip File References.
13. **HANDOFF**: Physical or network location of camera card files and session log.
14. **RESPONSIBLE ROLE**: Studio Recording Operator.
15. **BUSINESS RULES**: Presenter must strictly deliver the verified script without ad-libbing mathematical inaccuracies; audio must be free of clipping, hum, and echo.

---

### STEP 05 — Raw Footage Handoff

1. **PURPOSE**: Securely transfer raw camera and microphone files into the central digital asset repository and verify data integrity before card clearance.
2. **ACTOR / OWNER**: Studio Recording Operator / Ingest Assistant.
3. **ENTRY CONDITION**: Step 04 complete; Recording session concluded.
4. **ENTRY POINT**: Asset Ingestion Portal.
5. **INPUTS**: Camera raw video files, separate audio tracks (if dual-system), Recording Session Log.
6. **USER ACTIONS**: Ingest raw media files into designated central repository folders, perform checksum/playback integrity check, link raw asset URIs to the Production Item, and notify post-production.
7. **OUTPUT**: Verified Ingested Raw Footage Asset Record.
8. **COMPLETION CONDITION**: File sizes match, files play cleanly without corruption, and storage path is permanently cataloged in the Production Item.
9. **NEXT STEP**: Step 06 (Editing).
10. **REVISION / REJECTION**: If transfer fails or file is corrupted, re-ingest from physical card. If card was cleared or file lost, item fails back to Step 04 for re-recording.
11. **DATA CONSUMED**: Raw camera media files, Session Take Log.
12. **DATA PRODUCED**: Raw Asset Manifest, Storage Location URI, File Checksum.
13. **HANDOFF**: Verified Raw Asset Package available to the video editor.
14. **RESPONSIBLE ROLE**: Ingest Assistant.
15. **BUSINESS RULES**: Raw camera media must never be wiped from physical cards until Raw Footage Handoff is fully validated and stored in redundant storage.

---

### STEP 06 — Editing

1. **PURPOSE**: Assemble raw footage into a fast-paced, broadcast-standard educational video with motion graphics, sound design, and burned-in captions.
2. **ACTOR / OWNER**: Video Editor.
3. **ENTRY CONDITION**: Step 05 complete; Raw footage assets verified in central storage.
4. **ENTRY POINT**: Video Editing Workboard.
5. **INPUTS**: Raw Hero Take Video/Audio, Locked Script with Visual Cues, Brand Motion Graphic Templates.
6. **USER ACTIONS**: Rough cut assembly, pacing tightening (remove pauses), color grading, on-screen text/graphic generation matching question text, subtitle synchronization, sound effects addition, loudness normalization, and render master video.
7. **OUTPUT**: Rendered Master Video File.
8. **COMPLETION CONDITION**: Complete video rendered to technical specification (resolution, bitrate, -14 LUFS audio, frame rate) and uploaded to the review destination.
9. **NEXT STEP**: Step 07 (Final QC).
10. **REVISION / REJECTION**: Internal revisions occur within Step 06 prior to QC handoff.
11. **DATA CONSUMED**: Raw Footage, Locked Script, Visual Guidelines.
12. **DATA PRODUCED**: Master Edited Video Record, Render File URI, Video Duration.
13. **HANDOFF**: Rendered Master Video ready for Quality Control inspection.
14. **RESPONSIBLE ROLE**: Video Editor.
15. **BUSINESS RULES**: On-screen graphics displaying formulas or question choices must exactly match the verified question text character-for-character; captions must be 100% synchronized with spoken audio.

---

### STEP 07 — Final QC

1. **PURPOSE**: Execute an uncompromising technical, aesthetic, and academic evaluation of the rendered video before public-facing assets are designed or released.
2. **ACTOR / OWNER**: Quality Control (QC) Specialist.
3. **ENTRY CONDITION**: Step 06 complete; Rendered Master Video available.
4. **ENTRY POINT**: Final QC Review Console.
5. **INPUTS**: Rendered Master Video, Verified Question Record, Technical QC Checklist.
6. **USER ACTIONS**: Inspect video for frame drops, audio sync drift, graphic typos, contrast issues, branding accuracy, and academic consistency. Submit formal QC evaluation.
7. **OUTPUT**: Final QC Sign-off Record (Passed or Failed with timestamped defect annotations).
8. **COMPLETION CONDITION**: QC Specialist explicitly marks all checklist items as passed and signs off on the release certificate.
9. **NEXT STEP**: Step 08 (Thumbnail).
10. **REVISION / REJECTION**: Failure immediately routes the item back to Step 06 (Editing) with timecoded repair notes. If an unrecoverable filming error is spotted, escalates back to Step 04 (Recording).
11. **DATA CONSUMED**: Rendered Master Video, Verified Question Data.
12. **DATA PRODUCED**: QC Approval Certificate, Defect Audit Trail.
13. **HANDOFF**: QC-Certified Video Master unlocked for packaging.
14. **RESPONSIBLE ROLE**: Quality Control (QC) Specialist.
15. **BUSINESS RULES**: No video may bypass Final QC under any operational urgency; any on-screen typo in question or answer results in mandatory rejection.

---

### STEP 08 — Thumbnail

1. **PURPOSE**: Create a visually compelling, mobile-readable thumbnail image engineered for high click-through rate (CTR).
2. **ACTOR / OWNER**: Thumbnail Designer.
3. **ENTRY CONDITION**: Step 07 complete (or initiated concurrently once script/question is locked, but finalized after QC approval).
4. **ENTRY POINT**: Thumbnail Design Workboard.
5. **INPUTS**: Verified Question Hook, Presenter High-Res Frame/Photo, Graphic Brand Presets.
6. **USER ACTIONS**: Select high-emotion presenter expression, compose high-contrast background, place concise hook text (3-5 words max), test readability at mobile thumbnail resolution, render graphic.
7. **OUTPUT**: Finalized Thumbnail Asset (JPG/PNG at required aspect ratios).
8. **COMPLETION CONDITION**: High-resolution graphic rendered, uploaded, and certified as meeting visual standards and legibility requirements.
9. **NEXT STEP**: Step 09 (Social Review).
10. **REVISION / REJECTION**: Internal redesign occurs within Step 08 if legibility or brand standards fail.
11. **DATA CONSUMED**: Question Hook, Video Still Frames, Brand Assets.
12. **DATA PRODUCED**: Thumbnail Asset URI, Hook Headline Text.
13. **HANDOFF**: Approved Thumbnail Asset ready for integration into the Social Review Package.
14. **RESPONSIBLE ROLE**: Thumbnail Designer.
15. **BUSINESS RULES**: Text on thumbnail must never spoil the correct answer; font size must remain legible on a 1.5-inch mobile screen.

---

### STEP 09 — Social Review

1. **PURPOSE**: Holistic editorial gatekeeper review assessing the combined package (Video + Thumbnail + Title + Caption + Pinned Comment) as a single consumer experience.
2. **ACTOR / OWNER**: Content Manager / Lead Editor.
3. **ENTRY CONDITION**: Step 07 (Final QC) and Step 08 (Thumbnail) complete.
4. **ENTRY POINT**: Social Review Executive Desk.
5. **INPUTS**: QC-Approved Video, Final Thumbnail, Proposed Titles/Captions, Pinned Comment Draft.
6. **USER ACTIONS**: Evaluate package coherence, engagement pull, tone-of-voice alignment, policy compliance, and overall social packaging. Approve for distribution or request specific changes.
7. **OUTPUT**: Social Packaging Approval Certificate.
8. **COMPLETION CONDITION**: Content Manager explicitly enters an approval decision for the complete social package.
9. **NEXT STEP**: Step 10 (Publishing Setup).
10. **REVISION / REJECTION**: If video needs adjustment, return to Step 06. If thumbnail needs adjustment, return to Step 08. If metadata needs adjustment, revise in Step 09.
11. **DATA CONSUMED**: QC-Approved Video, Thumbnail Image, Metadata Drafts.
12. **DATA PRODUCED**: Social Review Sign-off, Finalized Title, Caption Copy, Pinned Comment Text.
13. **HANDOFF**: Fully Approved Content Package ready for technical upload and scheduling.
14. **RESPONSIBLE ROLE**: Content Manager.
15. **BUSINESS RULES**: Approval must evaluate thumbnail and video side-by-side; clickbait that misleads or fails to deliver on the problem solution is forbidden.

---

### STEP 10 — Publishing Setup

1. **PURPOSE**: Stage, schedule, configure, and upload the finalized content package into distribution channels according to the strategic editorial calendar.
2. **ACTOR / OWNER**: Publishing Specialist / Social Media Operator.
3. **ENTRY CONDITION**: Step 09 complete; Social Review package approved.
4. **ENTRY POINT**: Publishing Operations Desk.
5. **INPUTS**: Complete Approved Content Package (Video file, Thumbnail, Title, Description, Tags, Pinned Comment, Target Publication Schedule).
6. **USER ACTIONS**: Configure platform metadata for YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook; upload master media; schedule target live release time; prepare first-comment payload.
7. **OUTPUT**: Scheduled Distribution Package with pending platform release identifiers.
8. **COMPLETION CONDITION**: Media uploaded to all destination platforms and scheduled in a verified pending state without platform processing warnings.
9. **NEXT STEP**: Step 11 (Published / Live Verification).
10. **REVISION / REJECTION**: If platform rejects upload (format error, copyright flag), re-encode or resolve in Step 10.
11. **DATA CONSUMED**: Approved Content Package, Publishing Schedule.
12. **DATA PRODUCED**: Platform Draft / Scheduled Post IDs, Publication Schedule Timestamps.
13. **HANDOFF**: Staged Platform Posts ready for automated or monitored public deployment.
14. **RESPONSIBLE ROLE**: Publishing Specialist.
15. **BUSINESS RULES**: All platforms must be scheduled in lockstep unless an explicit staggered strategy is designated; scheduled time must adhere to verified audience peak windows.

---

### STEP 11 — Published / Live Verification

1. **PURPOSE**: Verify that the scheduled content went live successfully, is publicly playable, has functional audio, correct thumbnail display, and active pinned comment.
2. **ACTOR / OWNER**: Publishing Specialist / Content Operations Auditor.
3. **ENTRY CONDITION**: Scheduled release timestamp has passed; platform states indicate post is public.
4. **ENTRY POINT**: Live Release Verification Dashboard.
5. **INPUTS**: Platform Scheduled Post IDs, Expected Live URLs.
6. **USER ACTIONS**: Access live public URL from an external network; verify video plays smoothly; check audio clarity on live feed; verify thumbnail and title rendering; post or verify pinned solution comment; log live status.
7. **OUTPUT**: Verified Live Release Certificate with validated canonical public URLs.
8. **COMPLETION CONDITION**: Auditor confirms physical public playback on all active distribution channels and records the live URLs.
9. **NEXT STEP**: Step 12 (Platform Sync / Package).
10. **REVISION / REJECTION**: If post failed to publish, got muted, or suffered aspect ratio distortion: execute immediate corrective publishing action (re-publish or replace asset via Step 10).
11. **DATA CONSUMED**: Platform Post IDs, Source Assets.
12. **DATA PRODUCED**: Verified Live URLs, Exact Public Launch Timestamp, Initial Playback Health Check.
13. **HANDOFF**: Confirmed Live Post record ready for cross-platform catalog synchronization.
14. **RESPONSIBLE ROLE**: Publishing Specialist.
15. **BUSINESS RULES**: A post is never marked "Live" solely because the schedule time passed; an operator or automated monitor must verify public HTTP playback.

---

### STEP 12 — Platform Sync / Package

1. **PURPOSE**: Synchronize cross-platform identities, bundle metadata linkages, and lock the distribution record across all syndicated channels into a unified production master.
2. **ACTOR / OWNER**: Publishing Specialist / Systems Coordinator.
3. **ENTRY CONDITION**: Step 11 complete; Public URLs verified.
4. **ENTRY POINT**: Syndication Management Hub.
5. **INPUTS**: Live URLs across YouTube, Instagram, Facebook; Master Production Item ID.
6. **USER ACTIONS**: Interlink foreign platform IDs (YouTube Video ID, Instagram Media ID, Facebook Post ID) to the core Production Item record; lock production state against further asset mutation.
7. **OUTPUT**: Unified Syndication Manifest linking all platform endpoints to the core Production Item.
8. **COMPLETION CONDITION**: All intended platform links are recorded, confirmed mapped, and asset status transitioned to `Production Complete`.
9. **NEXT STEP**: Step 13 (Social Analytics).
10. **REVISION / REJECTION**: If a channel syndication was missed, execute ad-hoc sync to complete the channel bundle.
11. **DATA CONSUMED**: Verified Live URLs, Platform Metadata.
12. **DATA PRODUCED**: Synchronized Channel Identity Map, Production Lock Flag.
13. **HANDOFF**: Locked, fully syndicated content record transitioning into the post-release measurement lifecycle.
14. **RESPONSIBLE ROLE**: Systems Coordinator.
15. **BUSINESS RULES**: The core Production Item cannot be modified or re-edited once the Syndication Manifest is locked.

---

### STEP 13 — Social Analytics

1. **PURPOSE**: Harvest and consolidate empirical audience engagement metrics, retention curves, and commentary data across all platforms over standardized time intervals.
2. **ACTOR / OWNER**: Analytics Specialist / Performance Auditor.
3. **ENTRY CONDITION**: Step 12 complete; Asset has been live for a minimum evaluation window (e.g., 24h, 7d, 30d).
4. **ENTRY POINT**: Social Telemetry Analytics Console.
5. **INPUTS**: Synchronized Channel Identity Map, Platform API Telemetry, Public Interaction Counts.
6. **USER ACTIONS**: Harvest views, watch time, Average Percentage Viewed (APV), Click-Through Rate (CTR), likes, shares, comments, and retention drop-off graphs; aggregate into normalized performance record.
7. **OUTPUT**: Consolidated Audience Telemetry Dossier.
8. **COMPLETION CONDITION**: Telemetry for standard observation intervals is fully harvested, validated against platform anomalies, and logged.
9. **NEXT STEP**: Step 14 (Performance Review).
10. **REVISION / REJECTION**: Incomplete or corrupted metric data requires re-polling platform data sources.
11. **DATA CONSUMED**: Platform Analytics APIs, Public Post Engagement.
12. **DATA PRODUCED**: Performance Metrics Dossier (Views, Watch Time, APV%, CTR%, Engagement Ratio, Drop-off Milestone Table).
13. **HANDOFF**: Normalized Telemetry Dossier ready for editorial evaluation.
14. **RESPONSIBLE ROLE**: Analytics Specialist.
15. **BUSINESS RULES**: Metrics must distinguish between initial 24-hour surge and long-tail evergreen views; data must be recorded without subjective weighting.

---

### STEP 14 — Performance Review

1. **PURPOSE**: Evaluate content performance against pedagogical objectives and channel benchmarks to determine editorial effectiveness.
2. **ACTOR / OWNER**: Content Strategist / Lead Editor.
3. **ENTRY CONDITION**: Step 13 complete; Consolidated Audience Telemetry Dossier available.
4. **ENTRY POINT**: Editorial Performance Review Room.
5. **INPUTS**: Consolidated Metrics Dossier, Original Question Record, Script, Video, Thumbnail.
6. **USER ACTIONS**: Compare metrics against topic/category benchmarks; analyze retention curve drop-offs against specific script moments; review top student comments for comprehension gaps; score overall content success.
7. **OUTPUT**: Qualitative & Quantitative Performance Assessment Report.
8. **COMPLETION CONDITION**: Content Strategist completes assessment questionnaire, categorizes performance outcome (Underperformer, Standard, Breakthrough, Viral Benchmark), and archives evaluation.
9. **NEXT STEP**: Step 15 (Performance Intelligence / Next Question).
10. **REVISION / REJECTION**: N/A (analytical assessment).
11. **DATA CONSUMED**: Telemetry Dossier, Production Asset Dossier.
12. **DATA PRODUCED**: Performance Rating, Retention Analysis Log, Student Sentiment/Confusion Classification.
13. **HANDOFF**: Completed Performance Assessment ready for strategic intelligence synthesis.
14. **RESPONSIBLE ROLE**: Content Strategist.
15. **BUSINESS RULES**: Analysis must correlate viewership drop-off directly to presentation techniques or mathematical clarity; success is evaluated on retention and comprehension, not views alone.

---

### STEP 15 — Performance Intelligence / Next Question

1. **PURPOSE**: Synthesize performance learnings across content clusters to formulate concrete, actionable directives for future curriculum generation, closing the production loop.
2. **ACTOR / OWNER**: Content Strategist / Curriculum Director.
3. **ENTRY CONDITION**: Step 14 complete; Performance Assessment Report finalized.
4. **ENTRY POINT**: Strategic Curriculum Intelligence Planning Board.
5. **INPUTS**: Performance Assessment Report, Category Performance Trends, Student Comment Inquiries/Confusion Patterns.
6. **USER ACTIONS**: Identify unaddressed student misconceptions; determine high-interest/high-difficulty topic areas; establish corrective scripting directives; issue structured "Next-Question Production Directives".
7. **OUTPUT**: Concrete Next-Question Directives (Topic, difficulty level, conceptual twist, hook pattern recommendation) fed into Step 01 backlog.
8. **COMPLETION CONDITION**: Strategic directive is formally created, prioritized, and linked into the Step 01 Question Generation Queue.
9. **NEXT STEP**: Step 01 (Question Generation) of new content items.
10. **REVISION / REJECTION**: N/A.
11. **DATA CONSUMED**: Performance Assessment, Historic Cluster Metrics.
12. **DATA PRODUCED**: Strategic Production Directive, Next-Question Specification, Topic Priority Ranking.
13. **HANDOFF**: Prioritized Next-Question Specification dispatched to Question Creators.
14. **RESPONSIBLE ROLE**: Curriculum Director.
15. **BUSINESS RULES**: Every low-retention or high-confusion topic must spawn either a remediation question or a revised explanation strategy; intelligence directives must explicitly reference empirical evidence from Step 13/14.

## 5. User Roles

To maintain an uncompromising, broadcast-standard production pipeline, eight distinct functional business roles are established. Each role embodies a specific skill set and operational responsibility.

### 1. Question Creator
- **Purpose**: Academic authoring and curriculum synthesis.
- **Responsibilities**: Generates syllabus-compliant, mathematically sound multiple-choice questions with thorough step-by-step proofs and intentional distractor options.
- **Steps They Can Perform**: Step 01 (Question Generation).

## 9. Step → Transition Map

### Normal Sequential Forward Path
`01 → 02 → 03 → 04 → 05 → 06 → 07 → 08 → 09 → 10 → 11 → 12 → 13 → 14 → 15 → [Seeds 01]`

### Legitimate Exception & Revision Loops

1. **Academic Verification Rejection (`02 → 01 → 02`)**:
   - *Condition*: Math calculation error, ambiguous wording, or wrong answer key discovered.
   - *Loop*: Step 02 logs structured defect notes → Item returns to Step 01 (Question Creator) for correction → Re-submitted to Step 02 for fresh verification.

2. **Script Pacing / Format Revision (`03 → 03`)**:
   - *Condition*: Script exceeds target duration, lacks strong hook, or has unnatural phrasing.
   - *Loop*: Handled entirely within Step 03 via internal redrafting before lock.

3. **Academic Defect Discovered in Scripting (`03 → 02`)**:
   - *Condition*: Script writer uncovers subtle flaw or edge case in verified problem during prose conversion.
   - *Loop*: Step 03 halts → Escalates back to Step 02 for re-evaluation.

4. **Recording Session Retake (`04 → 04`)**:
   - *Condition*: Presenter stumbles, audio pops, or delivery falls flat.
   - *Loop*: Re-recorded immediately within the studio session until an acceptable hero take is secured.

5. **Raw Footage Corruption / Ingest Failure (`05 → 04`)**:
   - *Condition*: Files corrupted during transfer and physical camera cards cleared/unreadable.
   - *Loop*: Re-filming required at Step 04.

6. **Final QC Failure (`07 → 06 → 07`)**:
   - *Condition*: Audio level violation, caption desync, on-screen formula typo, or graphic glitch.
   - *Loop*: Step 07 marks item `Rejected in QC` with timecodes → Returns to Step 06 (Video Editor) for surgical fix → Re-rendered and resubmitted to Step 07.

7. **Final QC Filming Defect Discovery (`07 → 04`)**:
   - *Condition*: Presenter mispronounced a crucial variable or skipped a calculation step unnoticed during filming.
   - *Loop*: Step 07 rejects → Escalates past editing directly back to Step 04 for pick-up recording.

8. **Thumbnail Revision (`08 → 08` or `09 → 08`)**:
   - *Condition*: Thumbnail text illegible on mobile screens, low contrast, or rejected during Step 09.
   - *Loop*: Re-designed within Step 08 to meet specified guidelines.

9. **Social Review Package Rejection (`09 → 06`, `09 → 08`, or `09 → 09`)**:
   - *Condition*: Complete package lacks cohesion, title doesn't match video payoff, or thumbnail feels disconnected.
   - *Loop*: Routed specifically to the responsible asset owner (Editing for video, Designer for thumbnail, or copy edit in Step 09) before returning to Step 09 for final sign-off.

10. **Publishing / Live Deployment Glitch (`11 → 10 → 11`)**:
    - *Condition*: Platform API failed to trigger public status, video muted for audio licensing, or aspect ratio crushed.
    - *Loop*: Publishing Specialist pulls post → Fixes upload settings in Step 10 → Re-verifies live in Step 11.

## 10. Workflow States

Every Production Item maintains an explicit business state that reflects its exact standing in the manufacturing line.

### Defined Business States

1. **`DRAFT`**
   - *Meaning*: Question is actively being authored or edited; incomplete.
   - *Set By*: Question Creator.
   - *Operational Implication*: Invisible to studio; held in draft backlog.
   - *Allowed Next States*: `PENDING_VERIFICATION`.

2. **`PENDING_VERIFICATION`**
   - *Meaning*: Question is authored, locked by creator, and queued for academic audit.
   - *Set By*: Question Creator.
   - *Operational Implication*: Appears in Academic Reviewer workboard; creator cannot edit.
   - *Allowed Next States*: `VERIFIED`, `REJECTED_ACADEMIC`.

3. **`REJECTED_ACADEMIC`**
   - *Meaning*: Academic Reviewer identified mathematical, factual, or syllabus defects.
   - *Set By*: Academic Reviewer.
   - *Operational Implication*: Returned to Question Creator queue with required fixes; blocked from production.
   - *Allowed Next States*: `DRAFT`, `PENDING_VERIFICATION`.

4. **`VERIFIED`**
   - *Meaning*: Problem certified as academically accurate, single correct answer, syllabus aligned.
   - *Set By*: Academic Reviewer.
   - *Operational Implication*: Enters production queue; unlocked for Script Writer.
   - *Allowed Next States*: `SCRIPTING`.

5. **`SCRIPTING`**
   - *Meaning*: Teleprompter script and visual cue plan are actively in development.
   - *Set By*: Script Writer.
   - *Operational Implication*: Assigned to specific writer; studio prep begins.
   - *Allowed Next States*: `SCRIPT_LOCKED`, `PENDING_VERIFICATION` (if defect found).

6. **`SCRIPT_LOCKED`**
   - *Meaning*: Spoken copy, hook, and visual directives finalized; ready for prompter.
   - *Set By*: Script Writer / Content Manager.
   - *Operational Implication*: Prompter files loaded; filming session can be scheduled.
   - *Allowed Next States*: `RECORDING`.

7. **`RECORDING`**
   - *Meaning*: In the studio or on schedule for filming.
   - *Set By*: Studio Recording Operator.
   - *Operational Implication*: Studio resources allocated.
   - *Allowed Next States*: `RECORDED`, `SCRIPT_LOCKED` (if rescheduled).

8. **`RECORDED`**
   - *Meaning*: Presenter has filmed acceptable takes; camera session wrapped.
   - *Set By*: Studio Recording Operator.
   - *Operational Implication*: Media resides on physical cards awaiting ingest.
   - *Allowed Next States*: `RAW_INGESTED`.

9. **`RAW_INGESTED`**
   - *Meaning*: Camera/audio files securely transferred to central repository with checksums verified.
   - *Set By*: Ingest Assistant.
   - *Operational Implication*: Camera cards can be wiped; assets unlocked for Video Editor.
   - *Allowed Next States*: `EDITING`.

10. **`EDITING`**
    - *Meaning*: Post-production video assembly, graphics, subtitles, and audio leveling in progress.
    - *Set By*: Video Editor.
    - *Operational Implication*: In editor workspace.
    - *Allowed Next States*: `PENDING_QC`.

11. **`PENDING_QC`**
    - *Meaning*: Master video rendered and submitted to quality assurance.
    - *Set By*: Video Editor.
    - *Operational Implication*: Video locked from editor edits; queued for QC Specialist.
    - *Allowed Next States*: `QC_APPROVED`, `REJECTED_QC`.

12. **`REJECTED_QC`**
    - *Meaning*: Video failed technical, graphic, audio, or sync standards.
    - *Set By*: QC Specialist.
    - *Operational Implication*: Returns to Video Editor workboard with timecoded bug list.
    - *Allowed Next States*: `EDITING`, `RECORDING` (if fatal filming flaw).

13. **`QC_APPROVED`**
    - *Meaning*: Video certified as technically flawless and broadcast-ready.
    - *Set By*: QC Specialist.
    - *Operational Implication*: Video asset locked permanently; packaging stages unlocked.
    - *Allowed Next States*: `PACKAGING`.

14. **`PACKAGING`**
    - *Meaning*: Thumbnail creation, title drafting, social captioning, and pinned comment setup underway.
    - *Set By*: Content Manager / Thumbnail Designer.
    - *Operational Implication*: Creative components converging for final package.
    - *Allowed Next States*: `PENDING_SOCIAL_REVIEW`.

15. **`PENDING_SOCIAL_REVIEW`**
    - *Meaning*: Complete package (Video + Thumbnail + Copy + Pinned Comment) assembled for final release gate.
    - *Set By*: Content Manager.
    - *Operational Implication*: Queued on Content Manager executive review desk.
    - *Allowed Next States*: `APPROVED_FOR_RELEASE`, `PACKAGING`, `EDITING`.

16. **`APPROVED_FOR_RELEASE`**
    - *Meaning*: Complete package formally signed off for public distribution.
    - *Set By*: Content Manager.
    - *Operational Implication*: Handed over to Publishing Specialist for staging.
    - *Allowed Next States*: `SCHEDULED`.

17. **`SCHEDULED`**
    - *Meaning*: Uploaded to platform endpoints with designated future release timestamp.
    - *Set By*: Publishing Specialist.
    - *Operational Implication*: Staged in external platform queues.
    - *Allowed Next States*: `LIVE_VERIFIED`, `PUBLISHING_FAILED`.

18. **`PUBLISHING_FAILED`**
    - *Meaning*: Platform API rejection, copyright strike, transcode failure, or schedule missed.
    - *Set By*: Publishing Specialist / System Monitor.
    - *Operational Implication*: High-priority operational triage.
    - *Allowed Next States*: `APPROVED_FOR_RELEASE`, `SCHEDULED`.

19. **`LIVE_VERIFIED`**
    - *Meaning*: Public URL accessed, verified actively playing with sound, correct thumbnail, and pinned comment.
    - *Set By*: Publishing Specialist.
    - *Operational Implication*: Publicly available; production milestone reached.
    - *Allowed Next States*: `SYNDICATED`.

20. **`SYNDICATED`**
    - *Meaning*: All designated distribution channels mapped and linked to core item; item fully locked.
    - *Set By*: Systems Coordinator.
    - *Operational Implication*: "Production Complete". Transferred to post-release analytics lifecycle.
    - *Allowed Next States*: `HARVESTING_ANALYTICS`.

21. **`HARVESTING_ANALYTICS`**
    - *Meaning*: Post has matured past initial observation windows; performance telemetry being gathered.
    - *Set By*: Analytics Specialist.
    - *Operational Implication*: Telemetry pipelines collecting 24h/7d/30d metrics.
    - *Allowed Next States*: `PERFORMANCE_REVIEWED`.

22. **`PERFORMANCE_REVIEWED`**
    - *Meaning*: Editorial review completed; retention curves analyzed against question/script benchmarks.
    - *Set By*: Content Strategist.
    - *Operational Implication*: Performance assessment cataloged.
    - *Allowed Next States*: `INTELLIGENCE_CLOSED`.

23. **`INTELLIGENCE_CLOSED`**
    - *Meaning*: Insights, misconceptions, and directives synthesized and injected into Step 01 for future content.
    - *Set By*: Curriculum Director.
    - *Operational Implication*: Full lifecycle completed. Item archived as historical training and benchmark asset.
    - *Allowed Next States*: Terminal state.

## 11. Step Completion Rules

The golden production principle: **OPENING A WORKBOARD OR VIEWING AN ASSET NEVER COMPLETES A STEP.**

Every step requires a definitive, recorded operational action fulfilling strict completion conditions:

1. **Step 01 (Question Generation)**:
   - *Not Complete*: Creator drafted a stem but left options empty; or generated options but didn't write an explanation.
   - *Complete*: Creator populates stem, 4 distinct options, verified single correct key, full step-by-step proof, and explicitly clicks **"Submit for Verification"**.

2. **Step 02 (Question Verification)**:
   - *Not Complete*: Reviewer opened the question view and read the problem.
   - *Complete*: Reviewer checks all criteria checkboxes, enters independent calculation verification, and submits **"Approve Verification"** or **"Reject with Reasons"**.

3. **Step 03 (Script / Teleprompter)**:
   - *Not Complete*: Script Writer drafted spoken prose but omitted the visual cue layout.
   - *Complete*: Script Writer validates timing, includes all required visual cues, and signs off **"Lock Script for Teleprompter"**.

4. **Step 04 (Recording)**:
   - *Not Complete*: Presenter finished speaking on camera; recording session ended.
   - *Complete*: Studio Operator reviews playback, confirms clean audio and accurate delivery, logs the Hero Take ID, and marks **"Recording Session Completed"**.

5. **Step 05 (Raw Footage Handoff)**:
   - *Not Complete*: Files are uploading; or operator placed memory card on a desk.
   - *Complete*: Files finish copying to central storage, checksum matches source, media plays cleanly, and operator submits **"Raw Ingest Verified"**.

6. **Step 06 (Editing)**:
   - *Not Complete*: Editor rendered a draft and saved it to an internal scratch disk.
   - *Complete*: Master video rendered to exact export specifications, uploaded to the shared storage folder, and editor clicks **"Submit Video for Final QC"**.

7. **Step 07 (Final QC)**:
   - *Not Complete*: QC Specialist watched 30 seconds of the video.
   - *Complete*: QC Specialist reviews the entire video end-to-end against the formal QC rubric, checks zero-defect confirmation, and logs **"Final QC Passed"**.

8. **Step 08 (Thumbnail)**:
   - *Not Complete*: Designer created a PSD mockup.
   - *Complete*: Final high-resolution graphic rendered, checked against mobile contrast specs, uploaded, and marked **"Thumbnail Asset Approved"**.

9. **Step 09 (Social Review)**:
   - *Not Complete*: Content Manager glanced at the thumbnail or watched the video in isolation.
   - *Complete*: Content Manager views the combined package (video, thumbnail, copy, pinned comment) side-by-side and records **"Social Package Approved for Release"**.

10. **Step 10 (Publishing Setup)**:
    - *Not Complete*: Upload page was opened on social networks; or post is saved as an unvalidated draft.
    - *Complete*: Video, thumbnail, tags, and text uploaded to all target networks, scheduled for the target broadcast time, and operator submits **"Publishing Setup Staged"**.

11. **Step 11 (Published / Live Verification)**:
    - *Not Complete*: The scheduled clock time arrived; or platform dashboard says "Live" without independent playback verification.
    - *Complete*: Operator opens the public URLs from an independent connection, confirms public streaming playback and active sound, and submits **"Live URL Verified"**.

12. **Step 12 (Platform Sync / Package)**:
    - *Not Complete*: One platform URL is saved but others are pending.
    - *Complete*: All distribution platform IDs and URLs are mapped to the central Production Item, and system logs **"Syndication Package Locked"**.

13. **Step 13 (Social Analytics)**:
    - *Not Complete*: Analytics dashboard loaded.
    - *Complete*: Validated metric counters (views, retention, APV%, CTR%, interactions) harvested at scheduled observation milestones and saved into the Telemetry Dossier.

14. **Step 14 (Performance Review)**:
    - *Not Complete*: Content Strategist looked at view counts.
    - *Complete*: Strategist analyzes retention curve drop-offs against script moments, completes benchmark evaluation questionnaire, and records **"Performance Review Finalized"**.

15. **Step 15 (Performance Intelligence / Next Question)**:
    - *Not Complete*: General takeaways discussed verbally.
    - *Complete*: Strategist formulates a structured Next-Question Directive (specifying subtopic, difficulty tweak, and hook directive) and links it directly into Step 01's production queue.

## 12. Revision / Failure Model

When work fails a quality gate, the system responds deterministically according to five severity classifications:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       FAILURE SEVERITY TAXONOMY                         │
├─────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. RETRY        │ Immediate in-step repeat without state rollback       │
│ 2. REVISION     │ Targeted return to immediate predecessor step         │
│ 3. REJECTION    │ Gate failure rolling back to earlier manufacturing gate│
│ 4. BLOCKED      │ Environmental/hardware/API halt requiring triage      │
│ 5. ESCALATION   │ Upward referral to executive role for policy/dispute  │
└─────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Detailed Failure Pathways

1. **Question Fails Verification (Rejection)**:
   - *Detection*: Step 02 (Academic Reviewer).
   - *Action*: State changes to `REJECTED_ACADEMIC`. Item drops back to Step 01.
   - *Impact*: Creator must address written defects. Teleprompter script cannot be initiated.

2. **Presenter Delivery Flaw (Retry)**:
   - *Detection*: Step 04 (Studio Operator / Presenter).
   - *Action*: Presenter immediately re-records take in the same studio block. State remains `RECORDING`.

3. **Ingest Integrity Check Failure (Retry or Rejection)**:
   - *Detection*: Step 05 (Ingest Assistant).
   - *Action*: First retry copy from physical card. If card corrupted, transition state to `SCRIPT_LOCKED` and schedule re-shoot at Step 04.

4. **Video Fails Final QC (Rejection)**:
   - *Detection*: Step 07 (QC Specialist).
   - *Action*: State changes to `REJECTED_QC`. Detailed timecoded defect log sent to Video Editor. Item drops back to Step 06.
   - *Impact*: Thumbnail Designer and Publishing Specialist cannot proceed to Social Review until QC sign-off is achieved.

5. **Package Fails Social Review (Revision)**:
   - *Detection*: Step 09 (Content Manager).
   - *Action*: Targeted return. If thumbnail weak: return to Step 08. If pacing/video weak: return to Step 06. If title/copy weak: revised directly in Step 09.

6. **Platform Upload API Rejection / Copyright Flag (Blocked / Triage)**:
   - *Detection*: Step 10 (Publishing Specialist).
   - *Action*: State changes to `PUBLISHING_FAILED`. Specialist investigates audio licensing or encoding non-compliance. Re-encodes or swaps audio track before re-staging.

7. **Live Verification Playback Failure (Emergency Rejection)**:
   - *Detection*: Step 11 (Publishing Specialist / Auditor).
   - *Action*: If video is muted or corrupted on live platform: Specialist immediately pulls/privates post, enters `PUBLISHING_FAILED`, corrects issue in Step 10, and re-publishes.

8. **Unresolvable Academic Dispute (Escalation)**:
   - *Detection*: Step 02 or Step 03.
   - *Action*: Creator disputes Reviewer's rejection. Item escalated to Curriculum Director for binding adjudication.

### Phase Details

1. **Phase I: Academic Genesis (Steps 01 - 03)**
   - Curricular needs or prior intelligence spawn an academic problem.
   - The question is drafted, subjected to blind verification, and translated into an engaging spoken script with precise visual directions.

2. **Phase II: Studio & Post-Production (Steps 04 - 07)**
   - The verified script is performed on camera in the studio.
   - Raw media is verified, safeguarded into central storage, transformed by video editing into an on-screen educational presentation, and audited through an uncompromising Final QC gate.

3. **Phase III: Packaging & Distribution (Steps 08 - 12)**
   - The validated master video is paired with a mobile-optimized thumbnail and social metadata.
   - The complete package passes Social Review, is scheduled for publication, verified live on public networks, and cataloged into a locked syndication manifest.

4. **Phase IV: Closed-Loop Intelligence (Steps 13 - 15)**
   - The live asset accumulates real-world student interaction data.
   - Telemetry is harvested, evaluated against pedagogical benchmarks, and synthesized into strategic intelligence.
   - Step 15 formulates the next iteration of questions, injecting actionable directives back into Step 01 to start the cycle anew.

## 14. Performance Intelligence Loop

Step 15 is the brain of the continuous improvement cycle. It prevents the CMS from being a blind broadcast mill and turns it into an adaptive educational engine.

### The Six Strategic Questions Step 15 Must Answer:

1. **What happened?**
   - Empirical facts: Did the video reach target completion benchmarks? What was the 24-hour and 7-day retention profile?

2. **What performed well?**
   - Which specific problem types, presentation styles, visual overlays, or teleprompter hooks achieved >70% Average Percentage Viewed (APV)?

3. **What did not perform well?**
   - At what exact second did the retention curve experience steep drop-off? Did viewers abandon during the question setup, formula explanation, or final calculation?

4. **What patterns were observed?**
   - Are students consistently dropping off when algebra formulas are introduced without graphic breakdown? Do visual trick shortcuts outperform traditional textbook proofs by 3x in shares?

5. **What should be learned?**
   - Did student comments reveal confusion about why Option C was wrong? Did multiple students make the exact calculation error anticipated by Distractor Option B?

6. **How does that information influence future content?**
   - Step 15 issues a **Next-Question Production Directive** that directly dictates parameters for Step 01:
     - *Topic Mandate*: Focus on Ratio & Proportion (Remediation).
     - *Pedagogical Tweak*: Present the visual bar-model method instead of algebraic substitution.
     - *Hook Directive*: Pose the common misconception directly in the first 3 seconds: *"Why 90% of students pick Option B on this problem."*
     - *Distractor Tuning*: Deepen the explanation on Distractor B in the follow-up script.

## 15. Canonical Business Rules

The following business rules are absolute and must govern the operation of the system at all times:

1. **Two-Person Academic Rule**: No question may be verified by the individual who authored it. Question Creator and Academic Reviewer must be separate actors on any single Production Item.
2. **Strict Verification Precedence**: No filming, teleprompter loading, or editing may take place on an unverified or rejected question.
3. **Action-Gated Completion**: Opening a workboard, viewing an item, or letting a clock timer elapse never constitutes step completion. Completion requires an explicit, recorded operational submission.
4. **Physical Ingest Redundancy**: Physical camera memory cards must not be formatted or cleared until Step 05 verifies storage redundancy and file checksums.
5. **Universal Final QC Gate**: No video asset can bypass Step 07 (Final QC) under any circumstances, including operational rush or scheduling urgency.
6. **Zero-Tolerance On-Screen Typos**: Any typo in mathematical symbols, question numbers, option letters, or solution calculations on-screen constitutes an automatic, non-negotiable QC rejection.
7. **Side-by-Side Social Packaging**: Step 09 review must evaluate the master video and thumbnail side-by-side; approval of video without thumbnail or vice versa is forbidden.
8. **Independent Live Playback Proof**: A content item cannot be marked "Live" solely because its scheduled platform release time has passed. An operator or automated monitor must verify public playback from an external network connection.
9. **Production State Immutability**: Once an item is verified live and syndication-mapped (Step 12), the production assets (video, script, audio) are permanently locked against in-place modification.
10. **Closed-Loop Intelligence Mandate**: High-confusion or underperforming content items must spawn a corrective production directive in Step 15 to remediate student misunderstanding in future batches.

## 17. Open Product Decisions

The following business and operational policy decisions are intentionally documented for executive determination:

1. **Short-Form vs. Long-Form Workflow Alignment**:
   - *Current Stance*: The 15-step workflow is primarily optimized for high-velocity vertical micro-learning (<60s shorts/reels).
   - *Open Decision*: Does full-length comprehensive classroom video (15-45 minutes) follow this exact 15-step assembly line, or will long-form production require distinct sub-workflows inside Steps 03 (Scripting), 04 (Recording), and 06 (Editing)?

2. **Language Adaptation / Multi-Lingual Dubbing Trigger**:
   - *Current Stance*: Content is authored and produced in the primary target language (e.g., Telugu).
   - *Open Decision*: If a video is adapted into another language (e.g., English or Hindi), does it spawn a child Production Item starting at Step 03 (Scripting/Dubbing), or does it require a completely separate lifecycle starting at Step 01?

3. **Analytics Harvesting Cadence**:
   - *Current Stance*: Step 13 harvests at fixed intervals (24 hours, 7 days, 30 days).
   - *Open Decision*: What is the formal operational cut-off point after which an item's telemetry is permanently frozen for final Step 14/15 review?
