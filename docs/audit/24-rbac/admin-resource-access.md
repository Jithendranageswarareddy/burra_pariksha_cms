## 1. Resource Scope for Role ADMIN

- **Content Masters:** Full unrestricted read/write/archive access.
- **Questions:** Full authoring, verification, and editing access.
- **Videos:** Can reassign hosts, editors, force status transitions, and replace raw footage.
- **Publishing:** Can override Gate D checks and trigger direct publishing.
- **Snapshots & Users:** Sole role permitted to trigger database snapshots and restore operations.
